## 1. ON RRP rundown — Yellen / Fed

### Facility ownership / mechanics (Fed, not Treasury)
- FACT: Overnight Reverse Repo (ON RRP) is a Federal Reserve open-market tool. The NY Fed Desk conducts operations under FOMC authorization/direction; counterparties (incl. 2a-7 MMFs, banks, GSEs, primary dealers) park cash overnight against Treasuries. Source: NY Fed RRP FAQ (updated 2026-03-23) — https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/rrp_faq ; Fed Board ON RRP page — https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/overnight-reverse-repurchase-agreements.htm
- FACT (mechanics): An ON RRP shifts Fed liabilities from bank reserves to reverse-repo obligations while outstanding; SOMA size unchanged. Usage is proposition-driven by eligible counterparties at the FOMC-set offering rate (FAQ table as of 2026-03-23 lists offering rate 3.50%, counterparty limit $160B). Source: NY Fed RRP FAQ — https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/rrp_faq
- FACT (series identity): Daily take-up is published as FRED RRPONTSYD (NY Fed Temporary Open Market Operations; billions USD, NSA). Source: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/RRPONTSYD

### Treasury vs Fed — attribution hygiene
- FACT: Treasury issues bills/notes/bonds and manages the TGA; it does **not** operate the ON RRP facility. Facility offering rate / parameters are FOMC/Desk. Source: Fed Board ON RRP page; NY Fed FAQ (links above)
- FACT (related but distinct Treasury action often conflated): Heavy T-bill supply can compete with ON RRP for MMF cash when bill yields exceed the ON RRP offering rate — a market-relative-yield channel, not a Treasury “empty button.” No primary Fed/Treasury document fetched here orders a rundown of ON RRP by name under Yellen. Sources: facility mechanics (NY Fed FAQ); issuance is Treasury (refunding statements, e.g. https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0590)

Treat as **unverified slogan** unless a primary quote is later attached.
