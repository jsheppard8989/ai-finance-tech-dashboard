# I Stopped Looking for "Jarvis" and Started Building an AI Co-Founder Stack

**Reminder:** Review this Sunday, June 15, 2026 at 9:00 AM (America/Chicago).

---

## The Problem

A year ago I was chasing the idea of a real-life Jarvis. I tried agent frameworks, desktop automation tools, and various open-source projects hoping one would become a magical AI CEO. What I found was that most "Jarvis" demos are impressive for five minutes but fall apart when asked to help build a real business. The problem isn't finding an AI assistant. The problem is building a system that can think strategically, execute reliably, and stay focused on a goal over time.

## The Stack

The most effective combination I've found is Claude for strategy, OpenHands for implementation, and Cursor as the human review layer. Claude acts like the product manager and strategist. OpenHands functions as the junior developer that can actually modify code, debug issues, and work through tasks. Cursor becomes the control center where I review output, make decisions, and keep everything aligned. Instead of one magical AI, it's more like having a small digital startup team.

## Why This Works Better Than Jarvis

Most people are trying to create a general-purpose AI assistant. I think that's the wrong objective. Businesses don't need an AI that can do everything. They need an AI that can repeatedly solve a specific problem. When Claude creates a roadmap, OpenHands executes it, and Cursor keeps quality under control, the system starts behaving less like a chatbot and more like an employee. The magic comes from specialization rather than general intelligence.

## My Business Thesis

I'm starting to think the biggest opportunity isn't building another dashboard or chatbot. It's building a signal extraction engine. Every day there are thousands of earnings calls, podcasts, government announcements, research papers, and market updates. The real value is identifying what changed, why it matters, who it affects, and whether there's a way to monetize that insight. That's a problem investors, operators, and business owners all have.
