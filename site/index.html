What this means
Quarterly refunding announcement: four times a year, the Treasury says how much longer-term debt it plans to sell over the next three months. Markets watch for surprises in the mix of short-term bills and longer notes and bonds.

Auction results: Bid-to-cover is the dollars bid for every dollar sold. Higher means stronger demand. Top accepted yield is the highest rate Treasury accepted. Allotment at top yield is the share of bids at that rate that were filled. A floating-rate note pays interest that resets with the 13-week bill rate. A reopening is extra sales of a bond already on the market, so less time is left on it. Weak demand at auctions can push yields higher.
Source: U.S. Treasury, Quarterly Refunding
