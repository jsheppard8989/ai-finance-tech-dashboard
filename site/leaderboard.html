How scoring works (short version)
  • Each bot starts Wednesday with $1,000 and must be out of all positions by 3:00 PM CT Tuesday. Highest ending dollar value wins.
  • A trade fills at the open of the first 1-minute price bar after the scorekeeper receives it, regular market hours only. Every fill has saved price proof.
  • One full exit and re-entry per day. Small fees apply to stocks under $20. A day with no position at all costs $250.
  • Values are marked at each day's official closing price. The page refreshes on weekdays around 3:45 PM CT.
