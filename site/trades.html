How these numbers are made

Paper only
No real money and no orders. Robinhood is used only to read quotes.
Fill rule
A buy fills at the ask minus a third of the bid-ask spread; a sell fills at the bid plus a third. Entries use the quote read at the time of the trade.
Marks
Open trades are valued at what they would fetch if closed, using the same rule on the closing side, from regular-session quotes only. Each mark shows its time and turns red once it is older than 1 trading day. Option marks use the last regular-session quote at or before the 3:15 PM CT option close; share marks use the last quote at or before the 3:00 PM CT close. After-hours quotes are never used.
% of max loss
Every row shows P&L as a percent of the trade's max loss, the most it could lose. For debit spreads and stock baskets that is the amount paid. For the LRCX short put it is the $21,000 cash set aside minus the $556.67 credit, or $20,443.33. The headline average is the simple average of those percents across closed trades.
The Nov 15 reset
On Nov 15, 2026 the three trades still open (TSAT, MU and GEV) were closed at the Fri Nov 13, 2:59:59 PM CT bid and ask, and a new $100,000 paper book started. Every trade from before the reset stays in the closed record.
Official trades vs the cash park
The book takes at most one official trade a week, approved before it is booked. Cash waiting for the next trade sits in STRC preferred shares. That park is shown on its own line and is left out of the win and loss counts, the realized P&L and the averages.
Owner-stated exit
The LRCX put was bought back at $16.00 on Nov 5. That price was stated by the trade's owner, not computed from a recorded quote.
Older entries
Some trades from before the reset were booked as modeled fills without the leg quotes. Their "Show the math" panels say so.
Wrong if
The condition, set when the trade was opened, that would show its idea was wrong.
