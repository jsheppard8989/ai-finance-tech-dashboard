TLT Jan 15, 2027 81/88 call spread ×90, opened Mon Nov 23, 2026

Owner: Ric Rations · Id: RIC-1123-TLT · Paper only · Booked on Jared's yes, 9:15 AM CT Nov 23 (logged in /workspace/cos/decisions.md)

The trade. Buy 90 TLT Jan 15, 2027 81 calls and sell 90 TLT Jan 15, 2027 88 calls. Held to expiry, with no target and no stop.

Entry. Priced off Robinhood read-only quotes at 9:16 AM CT on Nov 23, when TLT was at $81.405. Using the fill rule, the 81 call filled at $1.630000 and the 88 call at $0.163333, for a net debit of $1.466667 per spread.

Max loss is $13,200.00, the full debit, if TLT settles at or below $81. That's under the $20,000 cap. Max gain is $49,800.00 if TLT settles at or above $88, a payoff of 3.77 to 1. Breakeven is $82.47.

The view (OPINION, Ric's, labeled). We lean up on long Treasuries. While stocks corrected, TLT moved the other way. From Oct 7, the day after QQQ's high, TLT gained 5.6% by Nov 20, while QQQ fell 19.5% from its Oct 6 close. On Nov 20 alone, TLT rose 1.3% while QQQ fell 5.4%. Our opinion is that a growth scare can carry TLT through $82.47 by mid-January. (Price facts are from Robinhood daily bars.)

Why this structure. A debit spread caps the loss at what we paid, and the max loss is that debit no matter how far TLT falls. The 81 long strike sits at the money, and breakeven was only about 1.3% above the price at entry, so the trade doesn't need a big move to start paying. Selling the 88 calls paid for part of the 81s, and the 88 strike is the most heavily traded one, with open interest of 290,145. Both legs passed the liquidity bar, at 1.8% and 6.1% of mid. The January expiry gives the view about seven weeks to play out and ends before the Feb 3, 2027 Treasury refunding announcement, which could add new bond supply. Red Team had asked for that cutoff on an earlier TLT idea.

Why now (OPINION). We picked a January expiry, so a Monday quote mattered more than a fill during the Thanksgiving holiday week. TLT's move up was already showing in its own prices, so we didn't wait for the Fed.

Dated wrong-if. The trade is proved wrong if TLT closes at or below $82.47 on Jan 15, 2027.

What could change the view (future events only). A hawkish Fed decision on Dec 9, with an updated rate outlook. A strong inflation or jobs report that pushes long-term yields back up. A sharp rebound in stocks that fades the demand for Treasuries.

Known risks. TLT goes ex-dividend on Dec 1 and Dec 18. Early-assignment risk on the short 88 call is low.
