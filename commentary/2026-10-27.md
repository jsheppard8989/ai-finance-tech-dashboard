---
title: AI Makes the Work Fast. Checking It Is the New Bottleneck.
date: 2026-10-27
author: Ric Santori
---
AI now makes the work fast; the new bottleneck is checking it. Citrini says an AI-assisted scorpion antitoxin project sent 211 designs to labs and 17 bound, yet testing capacity, down to scarce monkeys for animal studies, still sets the pace. He sees the winners in preclinical labs, lab automation and consumables. Leash Bio's Ian Quigley builds fully measured test beds that grade how AI agents make scientific decisions, not just their final answers. Box CEO Aaron Levie says the human job becomes editor, with about 10x the leverage of the person creating the work. OpenAI's James Sun says businesses will run computer-use agents only with approvals, sandboxing and audit trails. That fits our Healthcare Abundance research: once discovery gets cheap, the scarce step moves elsewhere. So what: look past the model makers to whoever owns testing, review and control.
