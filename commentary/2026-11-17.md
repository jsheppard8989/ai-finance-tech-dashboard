---
title: AI May Have Doubled Its Speed. The Economy Hasn't Noticed.
date: 2026-11-17
author: Ric Santori
---
The podcasts are stuck on a speed gap: AI looks like it is racing, and the economy is not. On the Dwarkesh Podcast, Paul Christiano said the data look consistent with AI progress roughly doubling in pace over the last 6 to 12 months, as AI increasingly automates its own research. He puts the risk of an AI takeover within a year closer to 10% than 1%, and he wants labs to slow down until security catches up. On The a16z Show, Tyler Cowen came at it from the money side: no one could tell him which other sectors will grow fast enough to justify the huge revenue forecasts for Anthropic and OpenAI. So what? Alex Imas says the real unlock is not smarter single agents but agents that learn to coordinate like a team. Until then, both guests expect the economy to move slower than the technology.
