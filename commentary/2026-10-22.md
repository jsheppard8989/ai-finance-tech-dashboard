---
title: AI's Problem Is Supply, Not Smarts
date: 2026-10-22
author: Ric Santori
---
AI is no longer short on brains; it is short on stuff. Dwarkesh guests say chipmaking tools, especially EUV, cap output growth closer to 2x than 10x. Moonshots points to power and data centers, with a compute shortage likely for three to four years. Modal's Erik Bernhardsson says clouds now demand three-plus-year upfront commitments, and he worries more about a GPU price collapse than oversupply. SF Compute's Evan Conrad wants a real forward curve for compute. Monetary Matters calls the recent AI selloff a credit panic, not the end of the buildout, even with CoreWeave bonds pricing roughly a 60% chance of default over several years. Nikesh Arora says 75% to 90% of tokens could move to cheaper open-weight models. Real use still lags: only about 5% of enterprises run agents outside engineering. Macro Voices adds diesel shortages and French debt stress.
