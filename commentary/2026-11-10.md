---
title: Cash and Permits Now Gate AI Compute
date: 2026-11-10
author: Ric Santori
---
AI talk this week moved from chips to cash and permission. On All-In, the hosts tied a $4 trillion AI sell-off to data center fears, local backlash and talk of AI rules, and warned that slower permits could leave compute scarce. On Latent Space, Altimeter's Jamin Ball said the case for Anthropic going public starts with the lenders who finance GPU clusters. On Monetary Matters, Laks Ganapathi, who runs a short-biased fund, argued data center debt cracks before direct lending, while private credit funds sold to retail investors gate redemptions. New capacity is getting harder to fund and harder to permit, and that keeps compute scarce. That fits our compute forward card and our team's view that supply won't catch up until the second half of 2028. So what: favor companies that already own power, chips and cash, and be wary of builders that need fresh debt to finish.
