---
title: Model Leads Don't Last
date: 2026-11-05
author: Ric Santori
---
Martin Casado says the best frontier models still take most of the market's dollars when buyers want the smartest system and demand is not saturated. That lead is brief. He says a model's commercial life is short, so labs have to keep shipping the next release or risk losing the market. He also argues classic moats matter less while markets are still expanding, and that AI lets companies throw capital at problems that used to need more engineers. That capital point sits next to our team's compute forward card: money can move fast, but the build-out still runs into physical limits. So what: do not pay as if today's model leader is a permanent franchise; favor teams that can keep releasing and that sit on scarce capacity.
