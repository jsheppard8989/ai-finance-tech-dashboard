---
title: AI Is Smart Enough. It Just Doesn't Know You Yet.
date: 2026-11-19
author: Ric Santori
---
The podcasts have found AI's real bottleneck, and it isn't brains. It's context. On All-In, Databricks CEO Ali Ghodsi said today's AI is already smart enough to transform work, yet most companies still use it as "a glorified Google search." The reason: the AI doesn't see the meetings, emails, docs and org charts where the real work lives. Even when companies feed it that context, he says, they run into security fears and token costs that keep climbing. On The a16z Show, Ken Liu of First Person Labs pitched the personal version of the fix: a portable "model of a person" that any agent can check, so you stop re-explaining yourself to every new tool. So what? Ghodsi expects closing the context gap at companies to take 10 to 15 years, and the payoff to show up in GDP only over decades.
