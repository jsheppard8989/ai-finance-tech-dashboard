---
title: AI Debt Got Cheap Enough to Buy. Not All of It.
date: 2026-11-26
author: Ric Santori
---
The AI selloff has bond buyers picking sides. DoubleLine's Robert Cohen told Bloomberg's Credit Edge he has switched from bearish to buying AI debt because "the prices and the yields reflect the underlying risk." His favorite setup is Amazon, Alphabet and Microsoft, companies he says deserve their AA ratings, now paying spreads he calls cheap for that quality. He now sees value in Oracle and SpaceX bonds too, after both repriced to junk-like spreads. But he draws a hard line at CoreWeave's unsecured debt, calling it "a bridge too far," because its GPUs go obsolete and must be replaced, and he doubts the capital structure can carry that. Economist Daniel Lacalle reaches the same split on MacroVoices. He says AI spending is not a bubble, but some companies will earn strong returns while others post losses, and the market is still sorting the winners from the losers.
