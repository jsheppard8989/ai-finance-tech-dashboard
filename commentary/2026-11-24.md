---
title: AI Just Handed Every Hacker Nation-State Tools
date: 2026-11-24
author: Ric Santori
---
AI has flattened the hacker pecking order. CrowdStrike CEO George Kurtz told All-In that today's models, paired with good harness engineering, give ordinary attackers capabilities close to a nation-state's, and can chain small, low-risk flaws into full remote control of a system. He says autonomous attack tools can fully compromise a company in under three hours. Defenders are stuck in what he calls "break or breach": rush a patch and risk an outage, or wait and risk a hack. Cognition CEO Scott Wu, maker of the Devin coding agent, puts a clock on it on Invest Like the Best: he expects major breaches at some of the world's largest companies and government agencies in the next six to 12 months. His answer is the same weapon pointed back, with defenders running swarms of AI agents that scan and monitor nonstop. Wu still thinks defense wins in the long run.
