---
title: Down 15% in a Week: Nvidia's Real Jensen Risk Is Speed
date: 2026-11-15
author: Ric Santori
---
Jensen Huang's exit from Nvidia is the story the podcasts can't drop. On All-In, the hosts noted the stock fell 15% in a week, with earnings days away. David Sacks argued the real damage is speed: Nvidia wins by shipping a new, far more powerful chip every year, Jensen drives that pace, and without him the company will "lose a tempo or two." The hosts split on whether China gains from any slowdown. On Moonshots, Alex Wissner-Gross floated a theory he called preliminary and speculative: the shake-up came just as the midterms reopened a path for the Remote Access Security Act, a bill that would make it harder for China to reach U.S. GPUs through cloud services, including in other countries. So what? Watch Washington. Wissner-Gross expects clarity in the coming months on whether that bill passes.
