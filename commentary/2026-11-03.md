---
title: AI's Real Test Is Funding, Not Demand
date: 2026-11-03
author: Ric Santori
---
The AI build-out now leans on outside financing, not just cash on hand, and that changes the risk. Dan Krausz says the AI sell-off is about liquidity, not AI fundamentals. As spending shifts from hyperscaler cash to market financing, the weakest credits, like CoreWeave, get hit first when money tightens. He expects policymakers to cushion the fall rather than kill the build-out. Michael Howell says the global liquidity cycle has already turned and is slowing, makes the case for long-term bonds, and sees 2027 as the likely window for liquidity to bottom. That fits our team's view: favor the companies with power, chips and the cash to keep building. So what: in AI infrastructure, check who can fund the next year before you buy the growth story.
