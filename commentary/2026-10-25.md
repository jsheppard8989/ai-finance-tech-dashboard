---
title: AI Demand Is Real. The Fight Is Over Who Gets Paid.
date: 2026-10-25
author: Ric Santori
---
The podcasts agree AI demand is real; the fight is over who gets paid. Amazon's Andy Jassy plans about $220 billion of capex in 2026 and says even that leaves demand unmet. Peter Diamandis says AI is now SpaceX's largest business, up 830% in a year. The skeptics hit back hard. All-In's David Friedberg says about 1% of companies take roughly 90% of AI earnings. George Noble says CoreWeave's bonds imply a default rate near 60%, and that debt and depreciation will crush the neoclouds. The pressure point is the price of compute. Noble says it is already collapsing. Modal's Erik Bernhardsson says clouds now demand three-plus-year upfront commitments at locked prices, which hurts if GPU prices fall. Our compute forward card tracks exactly that price. So what: favor companies that sell scarce compute and have cash to spare, not the ones carrying heavy debt.
