---
title: $12 to $4: The Price That Splits the AI Compute Debate
date: 2026-11-12
author: Ric Santori
---
One question splits the podcasts: is AI compute headed for a glut, or will it stay scarce? On Monetary Matters, Militia Capital founder David Orr argued compute is heading for a massive oversupply in the near term, though he expects AI to pay off long term. He said nobody but software companies is really buying it, and expects them to resell the extra. He saw offers for spare compute fall from about $12 an hour in September to about $4, a signal he admits is noisy since most big contracts are locked in. On The a16z Show, former Cisco executive Mike Volpi disagreed: chip supply, not power, is the real limit and likely won't loosen for two or three years, maybe five, so today's GPUs stay useful even if a correction changes who owns them. Orr calls that hourly price maybe the key variable for a collapse. Watch it.
