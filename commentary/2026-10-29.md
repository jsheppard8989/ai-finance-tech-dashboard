---
title: Compute Keeps Getting Pricier, and the Debt Is the Risk
date: 2026-10-29
author: Ric Santori
---
AI compute is getting more expensive, not cheaper. Silicon Data's Steve Hou says rental rates for H100, H200 and B300 chips are rising even though token prices have fallen since late May, a sign of strong demand and tight supply. He adds that the forward rental curve has climbed since October, which our compute forward card tracks. Anthropic's S-1 backs the demand side, with Jack Farley citing an implied 88% gross margin in Q2. Citrini disagrees. He warns that equity and debt now fund more of the buildout, making it reflexive, and that paid demand may lag model gains. He has rotated out of most chip stocks and is short some AI hardware, though still bullish on Nvidia. The Moonshots hosts see leveraged neoclouds washing out around 2027. So what for investors: chip rents say demand is real today, but sort AI infrastructure names by how they're funded.
