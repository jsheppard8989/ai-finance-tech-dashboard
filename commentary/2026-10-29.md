---
title: Compute Keeps Getting Pricier, and the Debt Is the Risk
date: 2026-10-29
author: Ric Santori
---
AI compute is getting more expensive, not cheaper. Silicon Data's Steve Hou says rental rates for H100, H200 and B300 chips are rising even though token prices have fallen since late May, a sign of strong demand and tight supply. He adds that the forward rental curve has climbed since October, which makes long lockups less attractive unless buyers get paid for them. That forward curve is exactly what our compute forward card tracks. Anthropic's S-1 backs the demand side, with Jack Farley citing an implied 88% gross margin in Q2. The danger sits in the financing. Citrini says more of the buildout now runs on equity and debt, and the Moonshots hosts warn that leveraged neoclouds could wash out around 2027, like the dark-fiber overbuild. So what for investors: demand looks solid, so sort AI infrastructure names by how they're funded, not just how busy their chips are.
