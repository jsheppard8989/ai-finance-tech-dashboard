---
title: Agents Love Everything That Made Linux Hard
date: 2026-11-22
author: Ric Santori
---
Agents are about to hit systems built for people, and the podcasts say the fix is a rebuild, not a new coat of paint. On The a16z Show, David Heinemeier Hansson said macOS and Windows are "unfit for the age of agents." The things that made Linux hard for people, the terminal, the config files and the cryptic error messages, are "the three things agents love." So he is building a Linux system for agents, and he says agents have been developing the whole thing for the past five or six months. On ChinaTalk, Luukas Ilves, Estonia's former government CIO, said governments face "agentic flooding," with FOIA requests and grant applications arriving in unprecedented numbers just as a generation of civil servants retires. So what? Ilves says the tech is not the hard part: "two-thirds to three-quarters of the work is process re-engineering, organizational shifts."
