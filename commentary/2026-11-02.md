Google's search business is the new worry. Peter Diamandis says Alphabet had its worst stock day, falling 13% and erasing about $500 billion, even as revenue grew to $127 billion. Alex Wissner-Gross predicts search ads top out in Q1 as AI agents drive more buying, and David Friedberg says AI answers may make ad clicks less effective, with Google Cloud as the major offset. Anthropic's S-1 tells the opposite story. Chamath Palihapitiya calls its roughly $80 billion loss a "phantom" accounting effect and puts gross margin around 85%. In finance, AI is arriving with humans still in charge. OpenAI's Joseph Kim says ChatGPT for Financial Services assumes "there's always a human in the loop," and Aman Singh says Arca's agents cannot move money on their own. Luke Gromen flags the other side: AI job losses could hit consumer credit, not just AI companies' debt.
