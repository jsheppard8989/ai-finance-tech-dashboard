Micron, the US memory-chip maker, closed at $934.27 on Oct 29, 2026 (FACT, [Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU/)). That is 4.50 times the profit analysts expect for fiscal 2028 ($207.80 a share on average, REPORTED, [Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU/analysis/)), so the market already expects profits to dip (INFERENCE).

Our view (OPINION): the drop will be deeper than that price allows for, because only about 13% of next year's revenue is locked in at contracted minimum prices and most of the rest rides memory prices. Our model puts the bottom at about $305 a share in late December 2028, 75% below the $1,213.56 high (ESTIMATE). Profits bottom later, in 2029.

This time looks worse than 2018 and 2022, when the stock fell 53.7% and 49.8%, because profit margins are far above any past peak and have further to fall. A drop the size of those two, to $562–609, would still earn a net profit on our paper trade.

We test the view with a paper put spread held to expiry on Dec 15, 2028, about 10 days before our modeled low.
