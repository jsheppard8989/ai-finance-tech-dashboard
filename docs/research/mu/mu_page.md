# Micron and the Memory Cycle

**How to read the labels.** FACT means it comes from a primary source such as Micron's own [SEC filings](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/mu-20260903.htm), linked on each line. REPORTED means a named secondary source such as [Yahoo analyst consensus](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU/analysis/), also linked. INFERENCE is our own arithmetic, shown. ESTIMATE is an assumption we spell out. OPINION is our judgment. UNVERIFIED means we could not confirm it.

**Main sources:** Micron's [Q4 FY26 press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000018/a2026q4ex991-pressrelease.htm) and [prepared remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf) (Sep 30, 2026), its [FY26 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/mu-20260903.htm) (filed Oct 9, 2026) with the [revenue and contracts note](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/R22.htm), and Yahoo's [price history](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU/history/) and [analyst consensus](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU/analysis/).

## Our view and paper trade

**The call (OPINION).** This memory cycle may stay tight for longer than any before it. But at today's price, Micron's stock already pays for profits staying too high for too long. Our model's base case: profits peak in the last quarter of fiscal 2028 (ending about Aug 31, 2028), and the stock bottoms in late December 2028 at about $305 a share. That is 75% below the cycle's highest close of $1,213.56 (Jun 25, 2026).

**The market already expects a dip.** Investors pay a low multiple because they expect profits to fall later (OPINION). The stock trades at under 5x two-year forward earnings on average estimates: $934.27 (Oct 29, 2026 close, FACT, [Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU/)) divided by fiscal 2028 consensus EPS of $207.80 is 4.50x (INFERENCE; consensus REPORTED, [Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU/analysis/), Oct 30, 2026). That holds while MU is below about $1,039; on the lowest fiscal 2028 estimate ($136.00) it is 6.87x (INFERENCE). Our claim is that the drop will be deeper than the price allows for, because most of Micron's revenue still rides spot and short-term contract memory prices (OPINION). About 13% of next year's revenue is locked in at contracted minimum prices: about $8.4B a quarter, against a $61.5B revenue guide for the current quarter. About a third sits under multi-year customer agreements, most with price bands; the rest floats with the market (INFERENCE; inputs FACT, [10-K note](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/R22.htm), [press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000018/a2026q4ex991-pressrelease.htm)).

### When and where the cycle bottoms: the math

**When profits peak (ESTIMATE).** Our model's base case puts the profit peak in fiscal 2028 (Sep 2027 to Aug 2028), because analyst consensus still has EPS rising from $176.58 in fiscal 2027 to $206.89 in fiscal 2028 (as of Oct 28, 2026; REPORTED, [Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU/analysis/)), and Micron expects supply and demand to be "much tighter in calendar 2027 and 2028 than they were in 2026" (FACT, [Micron remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)). The model puts the earnings low in fiscal 2029. Profits bottom in 2029; the stock bottoms earlier, in about late December 2028 (INFERENCE).

**When the stock bottoms (INFERENCE).**
- 2018 cycle: profits peaked in the quarter ended Aug 30, 2018 ($3.56 earnings per share under standard accounting rules, or GAAP); the stock's low was $29.02 on Dec 24, 2018, 3.8 months later (FACT, SEC filings and [Yahoo closes](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU/history/)).
- 2022 cycle: profits peaked in the quarter ended Jun 2, 2022 ($2.34); the stock's low was $48.88 on Sep 26, 2022, 3.8 months later (FACT, same sources).
- In both, the stock's low came 5.2 to 14.1 months before the earnings low (INFERENCE).
- Anchor: we measure from the revenue-peak quarter in every cycle. (In 2021–22 the highest net-income quarter was earlier, ended Sep 2, 2021, and from there the low came 12.8 months later; the four-month lag holds only on the revenue peak.) On month-end closes, the lowest month was 4 months after the peak in 2018 and 6 months after in 2022 (INFERENCE).
- Applied here (ESTIMATE/INFERENCE): the model's peak year is fiscal 2028, whose last quarter ends about Aug 31, 2028. Adding 3.8 months gives about Dec 25, 2028. That is 10 days after the paper trade's Dec 15, 2028 expiry; on month-end closes (4 and 6 months after) it would be December 2028 to February 2029. We state the date the math gives. The trade earns a net profit if MU is below $625.33 on Dec 15 while still falling.

**Where the stock bottoms (ESTIMATE/INFERENCE).**
- Trough earnings, base case: $4.57 GAAP EPS a quarter, or $18.28 a year (ESTIMATE, model: revenue falls 36.6% from a fiscal 2028 peak quarter of $79.7B, as it did in the FY2016 cycle).
- Multiple at a past trough: at the Dec 24, 2018 low ($29.02), the price was 9.4x the earnings of the next four quarters; at the May 13, 2016 low ($9.56), 17.1x (INFERENCE: our math from SEC releases and Yahoo closes; prices FACT, [Yahoo closes](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU/history/); EPS FACT, [Q2 FY20 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312520000030/a2020q2exhibit991-pres.htm) and [Q3 FY16 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312516000198/a2016q3exhibit991-pressrel.htm)). At the Sep 26, 2022 low the next four quarters were a loss, so no multiple applies.
- Earnings x multiple: $18.28 x 9.4 = about $172; $18.28 x 17.1 = about $313 (INFERENCE).
- The model's own number, step by step (ESTIMATE/INFERENCE):
  1. Price-to-sales: the median price-to-sales ratio at the past five lows is 0.72. Times fiscal 2028 consensus revenue of $318.9B, divided by 1.15B shares, gives about $200.
  2. Price-to-book: the median price-to-book at the past lows is 0.81. Times modeled fiscal 2028 book value per share of about $505 (ESTIMATE: a future figure, today's book plus consensus earnings for fiscal 2027 and 2028 less dividends, not today's book) gives about $409.
  3. The model gives no single central point, so we average the two methods' central values: ($200 + $409) ÷ 2 = $304.50, about $305.
- Cross-check: the earnings method above gives about $172–313, so $305 sits at its top end (INFERENCE).

**This time we expect worse than history (OPINION).** A bottom near $305 is a 75% fall from the $1,213.56 high, deeper than the 53.7% in 2018 and 49.8% in 2022 (INFERENCE). Two reasons. First, profits have further to fall: gross margin hit 86.8% last quarter, 25.8 points above the old record of 61.0% (FACT, [press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000018/a2026q4ex991-pressrelease.htm)), and Micron's CFO says even floor prices should give margins "meaningfully above any prior cycle peak margins" (FACT, [Micron remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)). Second, only about 13% of next year's revenue is locked in at contracted minimum prices (INFERENCE, [10-K note](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/R22.htm)); the rest moves with memory prices. The trade does not need us to be right about the depth: it only needs MU below $625.33 on Dec 15, 2028 and pays its maximum at $500, so a history-sized drop from the $1,213.56 high, to $562–609 (−54% to −50%), still earns a net profit even if our deeper bottom is wrong (INFERENCE).

**Checked against the market (INFERENCE).** At the paper-trade entry, MU was $957.72 and the $700/$500 put spread has a modeled original-entry debit of $74.666667 per share, setting expiry break-even at $625.33, 34.7% below that price. Our base case is well below it. The stock already fell 39.1% from $1,213.56 on Jun 25, 2026 to $739.00 on Jul 29, 2026 (FACT, [Yahoo closes](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU/history/)), and recovered; that was not the cycle low in our view (OPINION).

**Paper trade.** One contract of the Dec 15, 2028 $700/$500 put spread.

- Entry: buy one Dec 15, 2028 $700 put and sell one Dec 15, 2028 $500 put. The saved [Yahoo option chain](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU/options/) snapshot is Oct 29, 2026, 8:32 AM CT, with MU at $957.72. Original bid/asks: $700 put $108/$128; $500 put $40/$60 (FACT, saved snapshot). These are opening quotes, not later marks.
- Modeled original-entry fills (INFERENCE): buys at ask − (ask−bid)/3 and sells at bid + (ask−bid)/3. That gives $121.333333 for the long put and $46.666667 for the short put, a $74.666667 per-share debit. Calculations retain full precision until dollar totals are rounded to cents; these are paper-entry models, not actual execution fills.
- Liquidity caveat: both markets were $20 wide and the last trades were from the previous day (Oct 28: $700 put 1:00:13 PM CT; $500 put 2:15:06 PM CT). Modeled fills are not assured. The snapshot does not supply synchronized per-leg quote timestamps.
- Maximum loss: $7,466.67. Maximum net profit: $12,533.33. Maximum gross payout: $20,000 (INFERENCE).
- Break-even: $625.33 at expiry, rounded from $625.333333… (INFERENCE).
- Held to expiry on Dec 15, 2028. Current market values are tracked along the way, separately from the original entry. At expiry: a net profit below breakeven; zero at breakeven; a net loss above breakeven; the full debit lost at or above $700. The maximum net profit is earned at or below $500.
- Why it fits the call (INFERENCE): our $305 bottom is far below the $500 lower strike, so if MU is on its way there by Dec 15, 2028 the trade earns its maximum net profit of $12,533.33. The modeled low date (about Dec 25, 2028) falls just after expiry. The risk (INFERENCE): the trade settles at expiry. In 2018 and 2022 the stock rebounded 3% to 43% within three to six months of the low (FACT closes, [Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU/history/)). A low in mid-2028 followed by a normal rebound could leave MU above $625.33 on Dec 15, 2028 even if our bottom call was right. Our $305 base case gives a wide cushion; the bull stress test ($480–535) does not.

**Side note: bear case (ESTIMATE).** AI spending pauses as new supply lands in 2027; profits peak in fiscal 2027; the stock could fall to $53–63, near net cash after customer deposits of $49.10 a share. Treat it as a stress case.

**Side note: bull case (ESTIMATE, deliberate stress test).** Contracts limit the fall; the stock bottoms at $480–535. This case prices Micron's contracted volumes at a 61.0% gross margin, its prior record, even though CFO Mark Murphy said that "even at floor prices, we expect margins meaningfully above any prior cycle peak margins" (FACT, [Micron remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)). If he is right, this floor is higher. At $480–535 the spread is worth $165–200 a share at expiry, so the trade still pays most of its max gain if MU stays there on Dec 15, 2028 (INFERENCE).

**Side note: no downturn through fiscal 2028 (INFERENCE).** Holding 5.57x on fiscal 2028 consensus EPS of $206.89 gives about $1,152.

**The historical rhyme.** In 2018 MU fell from $62.62 on May 29 to $29.02 on Dec 24 (−53.7%). In 2022 it fell from $97.36 on Jan 14 to $48.88 on Sep 26 (−49.8%) (FACT, [Yahoo closes](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MU/history/)).

## Micron's contracts: real, but only a partial cushion

- Micron has 26 multi-year customer agreements, estimated at "over 35% of our revenue through 2030". About three-quarters of that revenue has a set pricing framework, mostly with minimum and maximum prices; the rest is priced "periodically based on market prices". Customer commitments total $32B, "the vast majority of which are cash deposits" (FACT, [Micron remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)).
- The 10-K says the agreements carry "binding commitments for specific contractually enforceable volumes over the multi-year contract terms" (FACT, [10-K note](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/R22.htm)).
- Contracted revenue still to be delivered was about $134B on Sep 3, 2026, with "approximately one-fourth" due in the next 12 months and all of it within five years. The 10-K says it is "not expected to be indicative of future revenue under these contracts" (FACT, [10-K note](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/R22.htm)). It reached about $150B by Sep 30 (FACT, [Micron remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)).
- CFO Mark Murphy, Sep 30, 2026, said the contracted amount is "based on committed volumes and minimum pricing and is inherently conservative", that "even at floor prices, we expect margins meaningfully above any prior cycle peak margins", and that he expects revenue to exceed the contracted amount over the life of the agreements (FACT, [Micron remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf))
- Risks the 10-K names: if customers fail to meet commitments, Micron "may need to enforce our contractual rights, which could result in litigation or disputes", and the supply built for these deals "could contribute to industry supply exceeding demand if customers were to breach their purchase obligations and if overall demand is lower than anticipated" (FACT, [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/mu-20260903.htm)).
- The $12.75B of deposits received in fiscal 2026 are "returned to the customer if the customer meets the minimum purchase commitments", and "nearly all" are due back between 2029 and 2031 (FACT, [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/mu-20260903.htm)).

**What that means in plain English (INFERENCE).** The contracted minimum is committed volume times the lowest contract price. About 13% of next year's revenue is locked in at contracted minimum prices: on the 10-K's timing, about $8.4B a quarter over the next year and about $6.3B a quarter after that, against Micron's $61.5B revenue guide for the current quarter. It is a minimum only if customers perform, and most revenue still moves with market prices. That is the core of our claim that the drop will be deeper than priced (OPINION).

## HBM and the other memory makers

- HBM is the stacked memory used in AI chips. Micron says its 2027 HBM deals carry "significant price increases year over year, narrowing the gross margin gap with conventional DRAM" (FACT, [Micron remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)). INFERENCE: HBM now earns a lower margin than regular DRAM, a change from fiscal 2024, when Micron called HBM "accretive to both DRAM and overall company gross margins" (FACT, [FY24 remarks](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_events/2024/09/Q4-2024-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)).
- HBM revenue share in Q2 2026 was SK hynix 50%, Samsung 33% and Micron 18%. Micron's share was 21% in each of the four quarters before (REPORTED, [Counterpoint Research](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/global-dram-and-hbm-market-share)).
- SK hynix had an 80% operating margin in Q3 2026, expects demand to exceed supply "through 2030", and says 2027 capital spending will "increase significantly" (FACT, [SK hynix](https://news.skhynix.com/en/q3-2026-business-results/)). It also expects PC and phone shipments to fall because of memory costs (REPORTED, [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/28/sk-hynix-skhy-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)).
- Samsung's memory business set records in Q3 2026 "amid capacity constraints", it "significantly scaled up sales of HBM4", and it expects full-year 2026 capital spending of about KRW 84.4T, with KRW 29T spent in Q3 alone (FACT, [Samsung release](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-third-quarter-2026-results)).
- Samsung's HBM4 revenue more than tripled in the quarter to about 60% of its HBM sales, and its 2027 HBM is sold out (REPORTED, [Asia Business Daily](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/enterprise-CEO/2026102911191454019)). Samsung expects the shortage to last into 2028 (REPORTED, [Edaily](https://en.edaily.co.kr/news/eda202610295404/)). INFERENCE: this is why we place the profit peak in fiscal 2028, not 2027, and why the cycle may run tighter for longer than any before.

## Our take: did AI break the memory cycle?

OPINION: No. AI has made this cycle longer and higher, and it may stay tight for longer than any before. Margins are 26 points above any past peak, customers have posted $32B of commitments, and multi-year customer agreements cover about a third of Micron's revenue, which is why our base-case trough stays profitable. But about 13% of next year's revenue is locked in at contracted minimum prices: the contracted minimum is about $8.4B a quarter over the next year and about $6.3B after that, against a $61.5B quarterly run rate. All three makers are adding capacity for 2027–28, and history says the stock bottoms about four months after profits peak, well before the earnings low. At today's price, the stock pays for profits staying too high for too long. Our model puts the low at about $305 in late December 2028.
